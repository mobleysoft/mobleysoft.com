# Blackhole Field Model

## Renderer Contract

`blackhole.source.js` is the immutable visual artifact. Its runtime copy must remain byte-identical with SHA-256 `417b9edc4e6e4c9c8857d96bbebb6ad305a516421b92e034b43a9c3627fe9f6d`.

Interpretations and gameplay belong on the independent `encounter-canvas`. They may observe or narrate the canonical field, but they must not replace its geometry, shaders, particles, camera, or animation loop.

## Abstract Machine

The artifact can be treated as a five-part generative field machine:

1. **Attractor**: a center or objective that changes nearby behavior.
2. **Transfer law**: distance becomes velocity, color, confidence, risk, or value.
3. **Propagating shells**: moving bands represent phase, generations, epochs, or signals.
4. **Mobile agents**: particles follow, resist, orbit, enter, and leave the field.
5. **Observer path**: the camera exposes how interpretation changes with viewpoint.

The visual output multiplies these systems rather than merely stacking them. That makes it suitable for domains where local behavior emerges from a shared global field.

## First Playable Interpretation

The first encounter treats the visible anomaly as a **multiversal-foam analog**, not as a claim about real cosmology:

- The exterior field is an observable boundary through which many vacuum states project.
- A survey pulse estimates spectral shear, branch density, and coherence.
- Matching the propagating surface phase opens a crossing without modifying the surface.
- The interior is rendered independently as connected, mobile vacuum cells.
- Interior contacts are provisional "causal antibodies": the manifold's response to an incompatible observer.

The anomaly remains unnamed. Interface labels such as `ANOMALY / EXTERIOR` are operational descriptions, not lore canon.
