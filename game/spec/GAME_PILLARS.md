# EVENTWAKE Game Pillars

These pillars are product constraints. A feature that violates one is rejected
even when it is technically impressive.

## 1. The Anomaly Is Canon

The selected `blackhole.js` is the exterior world, not a texture or concept to
reinterpret. Gameplay layers may observe it and add craft, instruments, and
effects on a separate canvas. They may not alter or reproduce its shader.

## 2. Piloting Creates the Timeline

Thrust, attitude, trajectory, and observation cause every transition. Timers may
measure an action, but no timer may pull the craft through the horizon or choose
a temporal frame for the player.

## 3. Read Light, Then Act

The surface is an instrument. Surfing improves scan resolution, discoveries
change the player's decision, and phase lock makes an insertion physically
possible. Scanning is never a decorative progress bar.

## 4. Time Is Traversable Space

Inside the anomaly, the player controls movement along a worldline. The player
must approach, brake at, and deliberately exit through a selected frame.

## 5. Consequences Remain

Travel creates a branch. The source history, earlier craft, failed attempts, and
carried information remain addressable. A restart is a player-facing tool, not
the fiction's explanation for erased consequences.

## 6. Mystery Is Earned

The interface reports observations and confidence. It does not explain the
anomaly before the player has gathered evidence.

## Product Test

The first release must let a new player state the experience in one sentence:

> I learned to surf a moving band on the cube, flew into the moment it showed
> me, came out before I arrived, and found my earlier ship still there.
