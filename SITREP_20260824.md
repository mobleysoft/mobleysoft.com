## Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: Scope Creep (Exosuit Interfaces)
- **Risk**: 10 interfaces could take 12+ weeks to fully implement
- **Mitigation**: Prioritize persistence + prompt engine (core); defer infrastructure interface
- **Timeline**: Phases 1-9 (prompt engine) = 11 weeks; phase 10 (recovery) flexible

### Risk 2: Project Discovery Ambiguity
- **Risk**: 300+ projects may have unclear parent ventures
- **Mitigation**: Human-in-loop curation; accept "shared infrastructure" status for ambiguous
- **Expected**: 80% high-confidence, 15% medium-confidence, 5% require manual review

### Risk 3: Claude API Consistency
- **Risk**: Intent extraction may vary between calls
- **Mitigation**: Temperature=0.3 (low randomness); validation layer catches edge cases
- **Fallback**: Multi-turn refinement for low-confidence results

### Risk 4: Performance Targets
- **Risk**: Persistence operations (create session, save checkpoint) may exceed targets
- **Mitigation**: Async artifact storage; indexed checkpoint retrieval
- **Monitoring**: Profile during implementation; adjust targets if needed
